Hey “home skillets”. Hey, sup? Howzit going?

Do you want to hit the beach this weekend? Totally! “Tubular”.
IRL (in real life) grief can wreak havoc on you, affecting your mind and heart and often your ability to function. You may become a “scrub”. You may be a bit “delulu”. IYKYK (If You Know You Know). But maybe that is the “solulu”.

*“No one ever told me that grief felt so like fear.” C.S Lewis.*

Grief creeps into your muscles, especially in areas like the shoulders, neck, and jaw. You may have been carrying this tension for so long that you don't even notice it anymore. But as you begin to release, you might feel these areas soften.

You may just want to be “chillin’ like a villain” at your “crib” all the time.

The benefits of having a healthy sleep pattern can be one of the most healing practices as sleep problems can exacerbate grief. You may not feel like eating or become licky-licky and overeat which contributes to a weakened immune system. Grief can make cooking difficult. Eat light meals that are hydrating and nutritious when you have little or no appetite. 

Healing is not forgetting. Sometimes you can be fearful of pursuing healthy healing patterns out of fear that you are "moving on" and then you may miss out on your “glow-up” (a major positive transformation physical or mental). 

Remember you need time to heal and continue with your life. But just acknowledge that you will never forget your loss. You may never "move on" to a place where you will not remember your loss. Sights, sounds, and smells will remind you. Memories will suddenly pop up when you least expect them. “Valid”.

Remember you are valued and **we need you here**. You are “in da clurb, we all fam”. You are not crazy. Okay, you may act “brazy” or a little “cray-cray” because you are grieving. I know there are moments when you feel like giving up, or you feel numb and everything feels utterly meaningless. You may have a “weird flex, but ok”. Unless someone explicitly says you have “negative aura” or -100 “aura points”, then it’s pretty straightforward and you can just keep going. If you do something “cringe” realise that there may be “Karens” who police your behaviour. People may ask you “what is the damage?” because they care about you. It is not the end of the world as we know it although it may feel like it. Ask yourself whether you are deranged. If the answer is a resounding “yebo yes” then you may have a “menty-b”. If you have a ment-b please reach out and get help.

**Please do not give up. You are not alone." Perhaps you just need to do something that “hits different” instead of “grimbling” around the house. Go to a movie or concert or wear “dupe” shoes (I swear I can’t even tell the difference!) that are “dripping”. Take a full “slaycation”. “That’s wig”. “Dibs” on the window seat.**

**You may have given up exercise and become a couch potato just vegin’, maxin’ and relaxin’.**

**You may do the “NPC” walk (move mechanically unaware of your surroundings). You may have  forgotten TFW (that feeling when) you’ve done your workout and had breakfast and it’s only 7am. “As if!” **

**“Big yikes” if you stop washing. Whoa, that’s harsh. Don’t be gnarly. That is not cool and it is a “red flag” sign that you need to get up. You are not a “wastoid” and you don’t want to get “grody to the max”. You don’t want to get “cancelled”. You want to be a “keeper” with all green flags. You may need to go out and make some “guap” at your “gig” and you don’t want to be “ick”. So take a shower. IYKYK. Life is all about L and W (lose and win). You got to have “rizz”. So dress up like “the whole meal” or like a “snack” and look “snatched”.**

Something as simple as basic self-care habits can have a profound effect on your overall well-being amidst grief and you can experience a lightening of your sorrow even if it is just for just a couple of seconds. 

“Touch grass”. Find a small motivation to get out of bed such as drinking some water, brushing your teeth, dressing to “slay”, going for a walk, bird watching, licking an ice-cream, gardening, “vibing” and getting “jiggy with it” to some “dope” music, “chilling” with some “homies”, having a cup of coffee or eating a healthy meal like an absolutely “bussin” omelette. “Word”.
