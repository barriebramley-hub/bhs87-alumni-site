### A Little About Andre

Andre the Hilarious Hypnotist (born Andre Grove) is a legendary South African stage hypnotist and comedian who has been entertaining audiences across Southern Africa and internationally since 1994. Known for his unique blend of theatre, comedy, and hypnosis, Andre turns everyday audience volunteers into the absolute stars of his unscripted, fast-paced live shows.

**The Show Experience: 'Planned Pandemonium'**

Andre’s signature style avoids standard mentalism or heavy-handed demonstrations. Instead, he uses a dry, witty sense of humour to guide volunteers through bizarre, side-splitting comedic scenarios.

- **Interactive Improvisation:** Because everything depends on the spontaneous actions of the participants, no two performances are ever identical.
- **Classic & New Routines:** Volunteers might find themselves convincingly acting like a washing machine, reacting to a sudden shark attack, losing the ability to remember their own name, or slipping into a full T-Rex impression at a trigger word.
- **Language & Tone:** He delivers his family-friendly acts in a fluid mix of English and Afrikaans, tailored to connect with South Africa's diverse crowds.

**Volunteering Rules & Ethics**

Andre maintains very strict guidelines to ensure his performances remain a safe, respectful, and entertaining experience for everyone:

- **Strictly Voluntary:** Nobody is ever forced, pressured, or singled out to come on stage.
- **Age Limit:** Only individuals aged 16 and older are allowed to step up and volunteer.
- **Safety First:** He explicitly excludes individuals who are under the influence of alcohol or experiencing certain psychological health conditions.
- **No Plants:** He addresses skepticism openly and guarantees that in over 30 years of performing, he has never hired or paid anyone to take part in the show.
