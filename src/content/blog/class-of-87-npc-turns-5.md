*Five years of looking after our own* ✨

On Monday 26 October, the BHS Class of 1987 Alumni Foundation turned five.

It began with a few classmates who saw a need and decided to act. They believed that the bonds we formed at Bryanston High should mean something when one of us hits a hard patch. Thank you for having that vision, and for doing the unglamorous work of turning an idea into a registered NPC that actually delivers.

Over the past five years, the Foundation has quietly stood beside classmates when life got hard. That help has come as financial relief and as practical, in-kind support. None of it would have happened without you. The Foundation is funded entirely by our own alumni. Every contribution, large or small, has landed somewhere it mattered.

A special word of thanks goes to our directors, David, Lance and Richard, who give their time freely alongside full-time careers.

So here’s to five years of Veritas, Fidelitas, Justitia in action, and to many more. 🥂

If you’d like to contribute, or know a classmate who could use a hand, please reach out to any of the directors. Everything is handled with care and in confidence.
