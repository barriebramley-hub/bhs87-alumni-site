For the better part of a decade the Class of 1987 has been held together by a WhatsApp group, three email chains and one person’s Christmas card list. All of them still work. None of them are searchable.

So here we are. A website, with a blog, written and collected by people who were in the same assembly hall in 1987 and have been arguing about what happened there ever since.

## What goes here

Stories from classmates. Links to the podcasts a few of us have started. Notices about reunions and get-togethers. And, in time, everything to do with the Class of 1987 Foundation NPC.
