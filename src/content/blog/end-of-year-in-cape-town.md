With the year drawing to a close, a few of the Cape Town contingent got together on Sunday, 8 November.

Paul, Craig, Marion, Kathy and her husband Mike joined me for a little hike to Sandy Bay. For the record, nobody stripped down.

Afterwards, Jane and Carol came through from the Winelands, and Trish joined us too, for a long, easy brunch.

It was a lovely, sunny, beautiful Cape Town day, and it was so good to see everyone. Here’s to the next one.
