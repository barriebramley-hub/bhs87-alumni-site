**ABOUT RIDE JOBURG**
The event showcases Johannesburg to the world by allowing cyclists to ride through the city’s streets, promoting cycling as a sport and a lifestyle.

The history of Ride Joburg began in 1997 as a local race. The race has evolved over the years into South Africa’s premier cycling event. There are tens of thousands of participants. The event takes place on closed city streets. Ride Joburg is also a platform for charitable fundraising through its “Ride with a Purpose” initiative. It has grown to include community programs like “Train Joburg” to support new cyclists. It is a platform for impact and significance since it promotes cycling culture. It connects communities. The event connects professional and amateur cyclists and has initiatives like the “Free Ride” to make the race more inclusive for lower-income South African cyclists.
