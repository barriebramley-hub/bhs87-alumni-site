**Tannie Tan’s Mock Lobster Recipe**
Mix chopped imitation crab sticks with chopped celery, chopped apple, plain yoghurt, mayonnaise, tablespoon of lemon juice, pinch of paprika, salt and pepper. Serve on crackers, halved tomatoes or lettuce leaves.

**Tannie Tan’s Tuna Recipe**
Mix tuna with mayonnaise, teaspoon of lemon juice, salt and pepper. Serve on toast.

**Tannie Tan’s “That’s so fire” Bean Dip**
Blend 1 can of butter beans, 1 can of chickpeas, 1 cup shredded white cheddar cheese, 1 cup cream cheese, 1 tablespoon chili powder and ¼ teaspoon cumin together. Add salt and pepper to taste. Squeeze a little lime juice for zing. Cook until heated through. Serve with a drizzle of maple syrup or honey. Serve with sliced cucumber and celery sticks.

**Tannie Tan’s Avocado Salsa**
Mash ripe avocado in a bowl with a fork. Flavour with lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.  Stir in a little extra-virgin olive oil. For a spicy kick you can add a dash of hot sauce. Add finely chopped tomato and red onion. Serve with toasted whole-grain bread.
